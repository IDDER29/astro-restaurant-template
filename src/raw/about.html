Our story

Good food,
any hour.

Marmara Restaurant started with one idea: that great food in Ben Guerir should be easy to grab — whether it’s breakfast before class or a burger long after midnight. Sandwiches, tacos, burgers, pizza, fresh salads and cold-pressed juice, all made to order. Dine in, take away, or have it delivered.

What we believe

Walking past at night, you should want to come in.

Warm, bright, alive — the smell of fresh food and the glow of the counter. That feeling is what we want the whole experience to be. Whether you find us on the street or on your phone, ordering should feel just as quick and just as inviting. The food is the product. The ease of getting it is the promise.
